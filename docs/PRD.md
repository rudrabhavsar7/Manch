## This Product Is called "Manch"

### The Thought Behind This

- The Sceniro here is that during a live gig artists often struggles in 
1. Keep In Sync With the rest of the fellow musicians 
    - What this means is that, when a singer decides a song other members need to find out and search for the song often in there phone or other devices or in their own notes and sometime in the live gig where the setlist is non-stop this feels like a struggle 
2. Find the lyrics / Chords / Structure 
    - what this means is that it is very likely that not all the band memebrs are aware about the lyrics 
    - Chords are also something which musician may need to find out 
    - Same goes for the structure of the song and where to start and end the song
3. Personal Notes / Annotations  
    - Notes can be anything like 
        1. where to add extra instrumental 
        2. In which part musician should play filler notes 
        3. Where their perosnal parts need to play etc.

### The Product Concept
- Create an App that helps musicians to perform better in live gigs 
- My Vision is that the app should have the following feature
1. The admin or we can say the lead have total control of gig they can either Create it or select from the songs, make a setlist then they host a gig , select setlist and share a qr code/a 4 digit pin to other musicians
2. Other musicians can join the gig by scanning the qr code/a 4 digit pin
3. Once joined the musicians can see the setlist
4. Other Musicians screen will be reflected according to the admin's screen in real time but with only limited controls, this means they can only do things like scroll up and down but can not edit or remove/reorder anything
5. Each musicians can annot there own notation but only they can see it 
6. The admin can see the setlist and add , remove , reorder songs 
7. The admin can see the setlist and can play the songs
8. another thought here is that setlist privacy 
    - what this means is that not every setlist is for every musician 
    - Some setlist can be private and some can be public 
    - Private setlist are those which are not shared with anyone and can only be accessed by the admin and the shared persone by admin
    - Public setlist are those which are shared with everyone and can be accessed by anyone who once connected through that admin's gig
    - The core objective is that even the public setlist can not be aces by the users of platform until and unless they join the gig 
    - This can be achieved by linking each setlist to a specific gig and the gig is linked to a specific admin 
9. So if a admin create a setlist he can decide to share it with other musicians or not 
10. other musicians can either join the gig by scanning the qr code or a 4 digit pin
